WITNESS & REMEMBRANCE

For difficult history

Some events deserve a different kind of portal.

Genocide, mass persecution, slavery, and other traumatic histories are not treated as score-based survival games here. These portals use timelines, documents, maps, testimony-style narratives, ordinary-life context, choices about witnessing and helping, and “What Really Happened” reflections.

🕯️ Individual StoriesFocus on people and families without turning their suffering into entertainment.
